# Textile composition proposals

Three authored contemporary adaptations, preserved from the initial visual proposal.
The integrated editor recipes and their saved parameters are in `examples/textile/`.
All SVG and PNG artworks have transparent backgrounds and contain no lettering.

- `wedding-cloth-frame`: layered border, alternating angular flowers and stepped scrolls,
  four corner rosettes and shoots pointing toward an open central field.
- `masmak-tiered-band`: a wide central ornament between independent narrow bands.
- `sara-paired-tailpiece`: an editorial adaptation of paired embroidered panels and fringe.

The geometry is newly drawn; these are not reproductions of particular museum objects.

Construction references:

- N. Yu. Kashpar, *Chuvash wedding kerchiefs*, Russian Museum of Ethnography,
  especially the borders and corner arrangements on pp. 148–153:
  https://www.ethnomuseum.ru/files/PDF/Library/Ornamentika/kashpar.pdf
- Paha Tere, historical headbands and embroidered costume details:
  https://www.paha-tere.ru/blogs/istoriya-paha-tere/ukrasheniya-chuvashey
- Tuimazy local history museum, sara belt ornament:
  https://ar.culture.ru/ru/subject/sara-sary

Regenerate with `node scripts/render-textile-sketches.mjs`.
The text-free horizontal presentation and monochrome/thumbnail reviews are in
`docs/textile-review/`.
